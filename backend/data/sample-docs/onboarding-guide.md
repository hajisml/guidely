# New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome! This guide covers your first weeks. Your buddy (assigned before day
one) is your go-to for anything not covered here.

## Before day one

HR sends your contract and equipment form two weeks before your start date.
Your laptop ships to arrive before your first day; if it hasn't arrived, tell
IT immediately at it-help@company.test.

## Day one

- 09:30 — HR welcome session (office, room Baobab, or the posted video link).
- Set up your laptop, email, Slack, and the password manager with the steps in
  the welcome email. 2FA setup is part of this — don't skip it.
- Your manager books a 1:1 to walk through your role and 30-day expectations.
- Join the #new-joiners channel and introduce yourself.

## First week

- Complete the mandatory trainings in the learning portal: security awareness,
  code of conduct, and data protection. All three are due within your first
  14 days.
- Meet your team: your manager schedules intro calls with everyone you'll work
  with regularly.
- Engineering hires: get repo access via the access request form, clone the
  main project, and aim to ship a small starter task in week one.

## First month

- 30-day check-in with your manager and HR — honest feedback both ways.
- Set your probation goals with your manager; probation lasts 3 months and
  ends with a review meeting.
- Pick one company-wide guild or interest group to join if you like.

## Practical bits

- Payroll runs on the 28th of each month; submit your bank details in the HR
  portal in week one.
- Office access cards are issued at the front desk; report lost cards to
  facilities within 24 hours.
- The hybrid work default is 2 office days a week after probation — see the
  Remote Work Policy for details.
- Questions nobody can answer end up with HR: hr@company.test.
