Leave Policy

Annual leave

All full-time employees are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per
calendar year. Leave accrues at 1.75 days per month of service. Unused leave can
be carried over into the next year, but carry-over is capped at 5 days and any
carried days must be used before the end of March.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 7 working days
in advance for leave longer than two days. Short leave (one or two days) needs
at least 48 hours notice. Your team lead approves requests; HR only steps in if
there is a scheduling conflict.

Sick leave

Employees get 14 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick days do not carry
over. If you run out of sick days, additional sick absence is deducted from
annual leave, and after that it becomes unpaid leave.

Parental leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave, and
secondary caregivers to 4 weeks. Parental leave can be taken in up to two
blocks within the first year after birth or adoption. Notify HR at least one
month before the planned start date.

Public holidays

The company observes all national public holidays. When a public holiday falls
on a weekend, the following Monday is taken as a substitute day off. Working on
a public holiday requires prior written approval from your manager and is
compensated with 1.5x time off in lieu.

Compassionate and study leave

Up to 5 days of paid compassionate leave is available in the event of the death
of an immediate family member. Employees enrolled in approved professional
courses can request up to 10 days of study leave per year; approval depends on
workload and requires proof of enrollment.
