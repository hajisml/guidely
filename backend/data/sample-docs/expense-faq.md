# Expense Reimbursement FAQ

**How do I submit an expense?**
Log into the expense portal, create a new claim, attach a photo or PDF of the
receipt, pick the right category, and submit. Claims go to your manager first
and then to finance.

**What is the deadline for submitting expenses?**
Within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims older than 30 days need a written
justification and finance director approval, and may be rejected.

**When do I get paid back?**
Approved claims are paid out with the next payroll run. Claims approved before
the 20th of the month are included in that month's salary; later approvals
roll into the next month.

**Do I always need a receipt?**
Yes, for anything above $10. For small cash expenses under $10 (parking
meters, tips) you can file without a receipt but must add a short note.
Bank/card statements alone are not accepted as receipts.

**What can I expense when traveling?**
Flights and hotels booked through the travel tool, local transport, and meals
up to the daily allowance of $50. Alcohol, minibar, and entertainment are not
reimbursable. Book economy class for flights under 6 hours.

**What about meals with clients?**
Client meals are reimbursable with no fixed cap, but must list the attendees
and business purpose on the claim. Anything above $100 per person needs
pre-approval from your director.

**Can I expense software or hardware?**
Software subscriptions and hardware must go through IT procurement, not
personal expense claims. The only exception is the $300 home office allowance
described in the Remote Work Policy.

**What happens if my claim is rejected?**
You get a notification with the reason. Fix the issue (wrong category, missing
receipt) and resubmit. If you disagree with a rejection, escalate to
finance@company.test within 14 days.

**Who approves what?**
Managers approve claims up to $500. Between $500 and $2,000 it goes to the
department director, and anything above $2,000 needs CFO sign-off.
