# Remote Work Policy

## Who can work remotely

All employees whose role does not require physical presence (front desk,
facilities, lab work) are eligible for hybrid remote work after completing
their 3-month probation period. Fully remote arrangements are possible for
engineering and support roles with director approval.

## Hybrid schedule

The default hybrid arrangement is a minimum of 2 office days per week, Tuesday
and Thursday being the company-wide anchor days. Teams may agree on different
anchor days, but every team must share at least one weekly office day.

## Working hours and availability

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time — you are expected to be reachable on
Slack and able to join calls during this window. Outside core hours you are
free to arrange your day, as long as you work your contracted hours and attend
scheduled meetings.

Update your Slack status and calendar when working remotely. If you will be
away from your desk for more than an hour during core hours, note it in your
team channel.

## Equipment and expenses

The company provides a laptop, and one external monitor for your home office
on request. A one-time home office allowance of $300 covers a desk, chair, or
other ergonomic equipment; submit receipts through the expense portal.
Internet costs are reimbursed up to $40 per month for fully remote employees.

## Working from abroad

Short stints of working from another country are allowed up to 30 calendar
days per year, subject to written approval from HR (tax and legal reasons).
Longer arrangements need a formal contract change. You remain responsible for
a stable connection and overlapping at least 4 hours with your team's core
hours.

## Security requirements

Remote work must follow the IT Security Guide: company VPN always on when on
public or shared Wi-Fi, disk encryption enabled, and screen locked when
unattended. Do not use personal devices for customer data.
